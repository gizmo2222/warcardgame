# Bug sweep and polish pass — November 2026

Sweep of `warcardgame.html` (last earlier work: 2026-10-08). The game was played end to end in Playwright (Chromium), served locally:

```powershell
python -m http.server 8791 --bind 127.0.0.1
```

Open `http://127.0.0.1:8791/warcardgame.html`.

## What was covered

- Full games to the end screen: manual flips, Auto Play at 1x–10x, and a 542-round auto game at 10x. The total stayed at 52 cards after every round.
- War edge cases, forced through the page state:
  - a normal war and chained wars
  - a short-handed player keeping their last card
  - a player who can't fight
  - both players out (draw)
  - New Game pressed mid-war
  - Auto toggled mid-war
- Phone width of 375px: no horizontal scroll. Taps work in a touch context (`hasTouch`, `isMobile`).
- Keyboard-only play, focus rings, contrast measured against sampled backgrounds, the accessibility tree, and `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
- Console: no errors after the fixes. Before them, the only error was a favicon 404.

## Findings, ranked, and fixes

Each fix is its own commit on `main`. None of them are pushed.

| # | Severity | Finding | Fix (commit) |
|---|----------|---------|--------------|
| 1 | High (keyboard) | During a war, Flip became `disabled`, so Chrome moved focus to `<body>`. The next Enter or Space did nothing, and keyboard players had to Tab back in after every war. | Flip uses `aria-disabled` and the click handler guards against auto play. Focus stays on Flip (`0b3b84a`). |
| 2 | High (keyboard / SR) | Game over: focus went to `<body>`, the overlay was not a dialog, and the buttons behind it stayed tabbable. Play Again used an inline `onclick`. | `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal` and labelled by the title. Play Again gets focus, the table behind is `inert`, Tab stays in the dialog, and focus returns to Flip afterwards (`ee75815`, `0d4f975`). |
| 3 | Medium (visual bug) | The WAR! pulse never animated, because a transform on an `inline` span has no effect. | `display: inline-block` (`1fc8906`). |
| 4 | Medium (a11y) | No reduced-motion support: the deal slide, the infinite pulse, the winner lift and the hover nudges always ran. | A `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` block turns them off (`3b7db22`). |
| 5 | Medium (SR) | Cards were read as bare glyphs ("Q ♠ ♠ Q ♠"), and the result message was not announced. The speed slider had no name. | Cards are `role="img"` with names such as "Queen of spades". The message is a `status` live region and includes the cards played. The slider has a `<label>`. The decorative war pile and placeholders are hidden from screen readers (`2818406`). |
| 6 | Medium (contrast) | Several texts fell below WCAG AA: player labels 3.7:1, stack counts 3.7:1, speed label 4.2:1, WAR! 2.2:1 (large text needs 3:1), the "+N" pile count 2.1:1, and Auto Play text 3.8:1. | New colours `#e6d8a6`, `#c8e0b0`, `#ffb3b3`, 85% white and `#f0f8e8`, which give 5.1–6.3, 6.0, 3.1–3.5, 4.3 and 4.7 (`0ef0b9c`). |
| 7 | Low (keyboard) | Focus rings were the thin browser default and hard to see on the gold Flip button. | A 3px white `:focus-visible` outline with an offset, 5.3:1 on the felt (`5d98e21`). |
| 8 | Low (touch) | The speed slider was 16px tall, below the 24px minimum. Auto Play was 32px tall. | The slider is now 28px tall and phone buttons have a 44px minimum height (`f5730ae`). |
| 9 | Low (polish) | The end screen said "Completed in 1 rounds", and the console showed a favicon 404. | Singular "round" for one round, plus an empty data-URI icon (`5162f74`). |

All of these were re-checked in Playwright after the fixes.

## Left as is

- The "flip" text in an empty card slot is at 15% white (1.3:1). It is a decorative placeholder and now hidden from screen readers. Raising it to AA (about 90% white) would make the empty slots loud, so that call is yours.
- The message line (`#f0e0a0`) measures about 4.5:1 against the lighter centre of the felt. It passes, but only just.

## Gameplay and design suggestions (not changed)

1. **Draws.** When both players run out during a war, the game ends 0–0 as "IT'S A DRAW" and the pot vanishes. The README says the game ends when one player holds all 52. You could split or return the pot, or note the draw in the README.
2. **Pot order.** A normal win shuffles the pot into the winner's deck, but a "can't fight" win adds it unshuffled. Pick one rule.
3. **War pile.** The face-down pile stays on screen after a war resolves, until the next flip. The count on chained wars also includes earlier face-up cards. Consider clearing it on resolve, and labelling it, e.g. "6 cards at stake".
4. **Losing card at 50% opacity.** This makes the loser's rank harder to read. A grey border or a smaller dim might read better.
5. **End screen.** The overlay is dark but the last two cards still show through behind the title. A more opaque backdrop, or hiding the slots, would look cleaner.
6. **Speed slider.** It only affects war pauses and Auto Play, which isn't obvious from "Speed". Consider labelling it "Auto speed" or showing the value, e.g. "5x".
7. **Shortcuts.** There is no key for Flip outside the button (for example F or Space anywhere). It is optional, since the button keeps focus now.
8. **Screen readers at high speed.** At 10x auto play the live region updates about 8 times a second. Polite announcements will lag or drop. You could announce only wars and the final result during Auto Play.
